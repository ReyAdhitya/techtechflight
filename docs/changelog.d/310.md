- **Lifetime hours per craft on Reports.** Accumulated Lesson time for each airframe that took off, across every closed Lesson.

DECISION: The Logbook has no per-flight airborne clock. A craft with `tally.flights > 0` in a closed Lesson is charged that Lesson's wall-clock (`endedAt − startedAt`). Open Lessons and crafts that never took off do not accumulate. Order is by `droneId`, not by hours.
